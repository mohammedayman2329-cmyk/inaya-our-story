Chapter one · 2023

The year it all began.

We were cousins, and our conversations started naturally — like two people getting to know each other as friends.

I was preparing for NEET. You were already working and attending classes. You were one year older than me, and there was something about the way you carried yourself that I noticed.

I admired you. Not because I was in love with you then, but because I genuinely respected the person you were.

Your hard work, your dedication, your maturity and the way you handled your responsibilities — I noticed all of it. You were beautiful, of course, but what stayed with me was your personality.

I never imagined that someone I initially admired would eventually become someone I would fall in love with.

Chapter two · A feeling taking shape

When admiration became something else.

As time passed, I went through a breakup in my previous relationship. My conversations with you continued, and slowly, a different kind of feeling began to grow.

I remember our video calls. I remember watching you groom yourself and get ready. Even those ordinary moments became special to me. I started noticing the little things.

One day, while you were sleeping during a video call, I quietly said something I thought you hadn't heard.

“I love you, Inaya. You're my dream girl.”
A thought I believed had stayed between me and the quiet.

Chapter three · The distance

Somewhere, we drifted.

Things slowly changed. Our conversations became less frequent, and eventually, you started ignoring me.

There was also the incident during my Mumbai trip. I shared something with Mahek about us meeting, and that information reached your ex. He became upset and came to your house, creating a difficult situation.

You were angry with me, and I apologised. You forgave me. The incident taught me an important lesson about trust, privacy and keeping personal matters between the people involved.

I also realised that I didn't always know how to manage my emotions. Eventually, we drifted apart. Sometimes you would message after a few months. Sometimes there was silence.

I didn't fully understand what was happening then. Looking back, I understand that you had your own reasons and circumstances. I don't want to rewrite the past or blame anyone.

Some experiences simply teach us things we couldn't have learned otherwise.

Chapter four · October 8

The second beginning.

Years passed. Then came October 8.

We started talking again. I finally asked why you had distanced yourself. You explained the circumstances surrounding your previous relationship, including your ex's behaviour and the concerns about being watched or misunderstood.

We began reconnecting. You asked for my photograph. I initially sent an AI-generated photo, but you asked for the real one. I sent it, and you complimented me. I asked for yours, and I complimented you too.

Our conversations slowly became regular. There were small misunderstandings about reply timings. Sometimes you waited for me while I assumed you had left the chat. I explained that I hadn't realised you were waiting.

Eventually, I started waiting for you too. Those conversations became part of my everyday happiness.

It felt like something familiar was returning, but this time we were getting to know each other differently.

“I started waiting for you too.”

Chapter six · A little patience

When happiness started feeling scary.

Eventually, you started feeling afraid. You told me that in your previous relationship, things had once seemed perfect, but eventually everything went wrong.

You were afraid that the same thing might happen again. I learned more about your past, including the difficulties involving your ex's family and the reasons you eventually decided to leave that relationship.

I understood that you were still healing. You wanted to become settled in life. You said it might take three years or even longer. I also have my own journey ahead. I may need around five years to become financially and personally settled.

I told you that I would wait until you felt ready. But I don't want this chapter to sound like a dramatic sacrifice or an obligation.

I want it to communicate something simple: I respect your healing. I respect your pace. I want both of us to grow.

We have already confessed that we love each other. We don't need to pretend those feelings aren't there. But we also don't need to rush our future.

“I don't want to rush your healing.”

Chapter seven · 2023 → 2026

The boy who admired you.

Sometimes I think about the Ayman from 2023.

Back then, I admired you for your maturity, your hard work and your personality. I didn't know how much you would eventually mean to me.

There is something else from 2023 that has stayed with me. When I was thinking about my future and internships, you were the first person I reached out to. I asked you what I should focus on, and you told me that, at the beginning, I should choose experience over money. That advice stayed with me — and I followed it.

About four months ago, when I got an internship, I messaged you first. I wanted you to be one of the first people to know. You didn't reply to that message, but I waited. Maybe because your opinion mattered to me, and because I wanted to share that little milestone with you.

Today, I know that we have shared feelings, memories, laughter, misunderstandings, private moments and a second beginning.

I cannot predict exactly where life will take us. I don't know what every tomorrow will look like.

But I am grateful that I got to know you in the way I do today. The person I admired then is someone I now know more deeply — and care for in a way I couldn't have imagined.
